## About me
My name is Maya Gomes. I am in the second year of my Master's Degree in Software Engineering at the University of Minho.

At the moment, I am developing my master’s thesis in the field of sentiment analysis, where I am leveraging transformer-based models, particularly BERT. My work focuses on applying and fine-tuning pre-trained language models to extract and classify sentiment from textual data, exploring different architectures and optimization strategies. 

In addition to my thesis, I am involved in the AMALIA (Creation of the Large-Scale Language Model of the Portuguese Language of Portugal) project at the University of Minho, where I am more recently part of the front-end development team.
